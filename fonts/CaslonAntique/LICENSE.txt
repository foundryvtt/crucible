==============================================
Caslon Antique
==============================================

The font files in this directory are a digitization of Caslon Antique, a typeface designed by Berne Nadall in 1894.
The digital font is Copyright Typographer Mediengestaltung 2000. It is not covered by the Crucible game system
license.

This font was obtained from https://www.fontspace.com/caslon-antique-font-f168 and added to Crucible on 2019-11-30.
That page lists its license as "Freeware."
